About us

We’ve been
where our clients are.

Rahim International is a real estate advisory and brokerage firm based in Kénitra. We help buyers, in Morocco and abroad, find the right property at the right price, and we help well-built projects reach a wider audience of serious buyers.

Our story

A bridge between good projects and well-informed buyers.

Rahim International was founded on a simple observation: in Morocco, good properties and well-informed buyers often fail to meet. We identified three reasons for this, and we experienced all three first-hand. The first is visibility. Many good developments, particularly during the construction stage, invest little in digital marketing and are presented only through a sales office on site. They remain largely invisible to buyers who live abroad or in another city, even when they offer excellent value.

The second is information. Transaction prices are rarely published, there is no reliable public database, and listings are often incomplete. Without local expertise, it is difficult to detect poor-quality construction, unreliable developers or inflated prices, and buyers are left to rely on what sellers choose to tell them.

The third, and the most important, is time. Buying property is often one of the largest purchases of a lifetime, and the right decision requires looking, comparing, researching, visiting several times and inspecting carefully. Even buying furniture or household appliances can take several weeks. At the souk, few people buy from the first stall they visit: they compare two or three, negotiate, and only once they understand the prices and the quality do they return, often to the first stall, and buy with confidence. Buyers who live abroad, or who work full-time in Morocco, rarely have the time to build this understanding of the property market.

What we experienced first-hand

These three gaps became clear to us through the experience of a relative of our founder, Ali Rahim. She lived in Canada and wanted to invest in an apartment in Kénitra. Ali had managed rental properties in the city for several years, and through this experience and his local network, he had already identified an off-plan apartment with a strong location, sound construction and a competitive price. Like many projects at that stage, it had almost no presence online and could only be seen at its sales office. From Canada, however, the project did not look convincing to his relative: the building was sixteen months from completion and the apartment was smaller than she expected. She therefore chose to continue her search on her own, through several weeks of research from abroad followed by a trip filled with visits.

Without time and without knowledge of the local market, she had no point of reference. She did not know what a fair price was, which practices were normal and which were warning signs, or whether an offer could be trusted. As her departure date approached, she came close to buying a larger apartment, on the assumption that more space meant more value. The transaction was cancelled shortly before signing, and she returned to Canada without having made a decision she could rely on.

The value of informed analysis

Ali then did what a buyer at a distance cannot do alone: he took the time to analyse the market for her. He redefined the selection criteria around her investment objective, reviewed each property she had considered and contacted the sellers directly. He found that several of them had quoted her higher prices than they offered local buyers, and that the larger apartment had weaknesses in construction and location that were not visible during a short visit. He then assessed the original off-plan project in detail, with repeated site visits, due diligence on the developer and a comparison with competing developments. The project she had first set aside proved to be the strongest investment available. She reserved the apartment off-plan with a single additional trip, and Ali visited the construction site every month until delivery, keeping her informed at each stage. Before she returned to Morocco to complete the final sale, he prepared her bank loan and her Daam Sakane application, so that everything was ready on time for the signing.

Why we are the bridge

This experience showed us that the problem is rarely a lack of opportunities. It is the distance, the missing information and the lack of time that separate good projects from the buyers who would value them. Rahim International was created to close that distance. We give good developments the visibility they lack, in Morocco and abroad. We give buyers the advice and information they need to make an informed decision with peace of mind and confidence, filtering out poorly built projects, unreliable developers and overpriced properties. And we give them time, by carrying out the research, visits and analysis that they cannot do themselves. From this experience, we developed a structured method and a communication system that keep every client informed at every step, whether they are in Kénitra or on another continent.

We bridge the distance between the right property and the right buyer.

Our name

Why a bridge?
