A project in Morocco, a clearer way forward

Sound familiar?

You fell in love with Morocco on a trip: the beaches, the souks, the sunsets, the evenings on a terrace… Back home, you keep browsing property listings. Whether to move here, visit more often or invest, the idea of having your own address in Morocco starts to feel serious.

You see other foreign buyers taking the plunge, cities developing and new projects taking shape. On social media, “Marrakech is the next Dubai”. You want to be part of it. For now, though, you mostly have a collection of pool villas in your saved listings and no idea what the first step should be.

Read more +

Where should you buy, with what budget and for what purpose? Is the neighbourhood you loved on holiday suitable for year-round living or renting out? Listings promise attractive returns, but with what charges, occupancy and management fees? You ask for figures. You get more beautiful photos.

Then come the questions specific to your situation. You are not a Moroccan citizen: can you buy this type of property? Which documents should you check before signing? How do you arrange financing, transfer your funds and keep the records needed for a future sale? Documents arrive in a language you do not always understand, and the answers change depending on who you ask.

You plan a trip for viewings. Some properties do not match the listings; others are no longer available. The one you like needs more checks, but the seller is already inviting you to reserve. Your return flight does not leave much time to understand the market and make a decision.

And after you leave? The bank is waiting for a document, the notary for a reply from the seller, and you try to keep track around your working day. Who brings the information together, explains what still needs doing and follows the purchase through to the keys? Even if you already rent in Morocco, knowing the country does not mean knowing how to buy property here.

Falling in love with Morocco made you want to buy. Turning that feeling into a sound project takes a strategy, verified information and a local point of contact who advises you at every step.

Your questions, our answers

Can a foreigner buy property in Morocco?

Yes, in urban areas. Agricultural land is a different matter, and we check what applies to your case.

How do I know the land title is clean?

Before any commitment, we check that the property has a land title, that it is registered in the seller’s name and free of charges that would block the sale, and we coordinate the final checks with the notary.

How do I transfer my funds, and get them back when I sell?

We guide you to a convertible dirham account, which lets you take your capital back out when you sell.

Can I get a mortgage as a non-resident?

Some Moroccan banks lend to non-residents. We compare their terms and help prepare your file.

What fees and taxes should I plan for?

We calculate your real budget, including notary fees, registration duties and taxes, before any offer.

How can I buy without being there?

We visit and film properties for you, coordinate with the notary and help arrange a power of attorney if needed.
