How we help

Depending on the service package you choose, our support covers:

  • Strategic adviceThe right project, budget and neighbourhoods, checked against the market.
  • Project comparisonLocation, finishes, price and developer, side by side, new builds and resale.
  • Visits togetherWe shortlist the best options and visit them with you, with a written report for each.
  • Return analysisRealistic rent, rental demand, charges and resale potential, if you are investing.
  • FinancingBorrowing capacity, Daam Sakane eligibility and the choice of bank.
  • Negotiation and support to the keysPrice, preliminary contract, notary, signing and handover of the keys.

What makes us different from a traditional agency

  • We start from your project, not from a portfolio.A traditional agency presents the properties it has been asked to sell. We start from your objectives and budget, then search the whole market, including new developments that are not advertised online and resale properties from many sources.
  • We analyse, compare and recommend.An agency’s role usually ends with the introduction to a seller. We research prices in the neighbourhood, check the construction and the developer’s record, compare the options and tell you clearly which one we would choose, and why.
  • We take the time you do not have.We do the searching, comparing and checking, so that you only visit the properties that are worth your time, and for off-plan purchases we follow the construction until delivery.
  • We stay with you until the keys.Financing, Daam Sakane, the notary and the signature are coordinated for you, and our communication system keeps you informed at every step, whether you are in Morocco or abroad.

Your questions, our answers

Where should I buy: Kénitra, Salé or Rabat?
We compare prices and demand in each city and neighbourhood based on your budget and how you will use the property.
Isn’t it cheaper to go straight to the developer?
The sales office works for the developer and only shows its own projects. With us, you compare several developers and the resale market, get an independent view of finishes and delivery, and have someone negotiating for you.
Is this price fair?
We compare every price with similar properties in the neighbourhood before any offer.
Is the developer reliable?
We check the projects they have delivered, whether they met their deadlines and the real quality of their finishes.
How much can I borrow?
We estimate your borrowing capacity and simulate your monthly payments before you start visiting.
Am I eligible for Daam Sakane?
We check your eligibility, and the property’s, before you count on it.
Is it a good investment?
We estimate the yield, the monthly income after the mortgage and the resale potential.
