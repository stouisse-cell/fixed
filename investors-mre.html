Moroccans Abroad (MRE)

Buying back home
shouldn’t feel foreign.

A home, a base for your return or an investment: we research the market, evaluate properties and coordinate your purchase from a distance, so you can decide with confidence without travelling back and forth.

Pre-Consultation Questionnaire5–10 minutes · no commitment

Sound familiar?

  • “You have to come and see.”Listings without a price or an address, and every question answered with a trip you can’t take.
  • A relative who has no timeA cousin visits on a Saturday and sends a blurry video. You don’t dare keep asking.
  • The “MRE price”The same flat, a higher price, because the buyer lives abroad.

Rahim International was born from exactly this experience. Our work is to fill the information gap, so you decide on facts.

How we help

Depending on the service package you choose, our support covers:

  • Strategic adviceWe define the right project, budget and neighbourhoods with you, and check them against the market.
  • Search and filmed visitsWe search the market, visit properties for you and film them, so you can see them as if you were there.
  • Analysis and recommendationWe compare the best options, figures included, and tell you which one we would choose, and why.
  • FinancingMortgage simulation, Daam Sakane guidance and banks used to working with MRE clients.
  • NegotiationWe negotiate the price and terms on your behalf, on your instructions.
  • Support to the keysNotary, power of attorney, signing and handover of the keys.

What makes us different from a traditional agency

  • We start from your project, not from a portfolio.A traditional agency presents the properties it has been asked to sell. We start from your objectives and budget, then search the whole market, including new developments that are not advertised online and resale properties from many sources.
  • We analyse, compare and recommend.An agency’s role usually ends with the introduction to a seller. We research prices in the neighbourhood, check the construction and the developer’s record, compare the options and tell you clearly which one we would choose, and why.
  • We take the time you do not have.We visit properties for you, return as often as necessary, film the visits so you can see them from abroad and, for off-plan purchases, follow the construction until delivery.
  • We stay with you until the keys.Financing, Daam Sakane, the notary and the signature are coordinated for you, and our communication system keeps you informed at every step, whether you are in Morocco or abroad.

Your questions, our answers

How do I choose without visiting?
We visit properties for you, film them and send you a written visit report, so you can compare at a distance.
Is the price fair?
We compare every price with similar properties in the neighbourhood before any offer.
Will I pay a higher “MRE price”?
Not with us negotiating for you. We compare the asking price with recent prices for similar properties nearby, and negotiate from there.
Can I get a mortgage or Daam Sakane from abroad?
Often, yes. We check your eligibility and guide your file towards banks used to working with MRE clients. Approval remains the bank’s decision.
Is the developer reliable?
We check the projects they have delivered, whether they met their deadlines and the real quality of their finishes.
How do I sign without travelling?
We coordinate with the notary, who will advise you on a power of attorney if you cannot be there.
