Moroccan Diaspora / MRE

Buying back home
shouldn't feel foreign.

A home, a return base or an investment: we search, visit and verify for you, and stay with you until you get the keys.

What we do for you

  • Strategic advicewe define the right project, budget and neighbourhoods with you.
  • Search and visitswe search, visit and film properties for you.
  • Analysis and recommendationwe compare the best options and tell you which one to choose, and why.
  • Financingmortgage, Daam Sakane, choice of bank.
  • Negotiationwe negotiate the price and terms on your behalf.
  • Support to the keysnotary, power of attorney, signing and handover of the keys.

Our method

  1. Strategyanalysis of your project, budget and criteria.
  2. Searchremote shortlisting and filmed visits.
  3. Analysiscomparison of the best properties and our recommendation.
  4. Negotiationprice, mortgage, notary and preliminary contract.
  5. Completiondeed of sale and handover of the keys.

Your questions, our answers

How do I choose without visiting?
We visit and film every property and send you a detailed visit report within 72 hours.
Is the price fair?
We compare every price with similar properties in the neighbourhood before any offer.
Can I get a mortgage or Daam Sakane from abroad?
We check your eligibility and prepare your file with banks used to working with MRE clients.
Is the developer reliable?
We check the projects they have delivered, whether they met their deadlines and the real quality of their finishes.
How do I sign without travelling?
We coordinate with the notary and arrange the power of attorney.
